## What it tests

_Tie it back to the validation brief: which assumption does this prototype put in front of a user?_

Hypothesis: we believe providing a fast, AI-driven scheduling assistant for online-lead submissions will cause more lead conversions for B2B Home Service Contractors. We'll know we're right when lead appointments increase by 40%, lead conversions (sales) increase by 25%..

## Context injected (no placeholders)

- **Real user quotes on screen:** "Sometimes I can' t be watching my phone or email and respond to a lead immediately when it comes in."
"I don't have enough in the budget to hire a call center that can be available for follow ups 24/7 within 10 seconds of every lead
- **Domain metrics on screen:** lead appointments increase, lead conversions (sales) increase.
