# FirstCall helps home services companies turn new leads into booked appointments through immediate AI texts, calls, and follow-ups.

_Product School · Vibe Coding Certification · Final Project Showcase_

**Live product:** https://instant-lead-booking-buddy.lovable.app

## The Problem & Hypothesis

**Problem.** Problem statement: Home-services contractors lose booked jobs because nobody responds to web leads within minutes, especially after hours or when the office is busy. Contractors need every lead contacted instantly and pushed to a booked appointment on a rep's calendar, without anyone on staff having to do it.

**Hypothesis.** The first contractor to reach a lead wins the job. Home services leads are usually submitted to several companies at once, and the one that responds while the homeowner is still thinking gets the appointment.

So the product bets on three things:

Speed is the whole game. A lead should be texted in under 10 seconds of hitting "submit" — not in an hour, not the next morning.
Texting alone isn't enough. A text gets read; a voice call right behind it gets answered. Both channels push toward the same goal: a booked time.
The booking has to be real. Every time offered is a time that is actually free on the assigned rep's schedule, so nothing gets double-booked and no one has to phone around to confirm.
If all three hold, more leads turn into booked appointments with no human touching the phone.

Friction: I struggled the most when having to review the code. I don't have a dev background, so I am not sure what I am supposed to be looking for. Outside of the prompts we were provided by this course, I don't know how I would have known to refactor the code. I also struggled with getting user feedback. The lack of analytics left me inputing more of my personal opinion rather than that of an actual potential consumer. 
Learning: Starting with little to no semblance of a plan is not helpful to build a working prototype or product. Mega prompts also can be a detractor by pigeon-holing yourself into a product that leaves little flexibility to adjust based on hypothesis evolution. 
Aha: Product management is truly key and valuable. You need people with the skills to plan and prioritize in order to produce a thoughtful, well-built product. (Sometimes it's difficult to remember our skills are valuable and we're not just paper-pushers.)
